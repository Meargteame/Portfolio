## Platform

## Users
Early-stage founders needing 0-to-1 SaaS MVPs shipped rapidly (2–4 weeks), as well as tech leads, agency owners, and high-growth engineering teams seeking elite full-stack contract talent.

## Product Purpose
Personal engineering portfolio and client acquisition engine for Meareg Teame. It exists to turn incoming visitors into qualified 15-minute discovery call bookings (via Cal.com), establish credibility through real shipped platforms, and clearly articulate the end-to-end engineering service offering.

## Positioning
Full-cycle product engineering: from initial concept through architecture, database/API design, frontend polish, and live production launch ("I turn promising ideas into Web Apps!"). Delivers with top 1% execution speed as an A2SV Fellow.

## Operating Context
Evaluated by technical founders, CTOs, and non-technical entrepreneurs seeking a dependable technical partner. Visitors inspect live case studies, verify architectural capabilities, check delivery timelines, and convert directly via one-click discovery call booking.

## Capabilities and Constraints
- Full-stack web architecture (React, Next.js, Go, Python, Tailwind CSS, PostgreSQL/MongoDB, Escrow APIs).
- Production MVP delivery in 2–4 weeks.
- Single-page fluid narrative with dedicated sub-routes (`/work`, `/services`, `/about`, `/contact`).
- Call booking exclusively via Cal.com (`https://cal.com/meareg/15min`).

## Brand Commitments
- **Name & Persona:** Meareg Teame — Full-Stack Web Engineer & Founder of Leons Lab, A2SV Fellow.

- **Visual Identity:** Modern high-contrast editorial craft inspired by clean architectural layouts (framed boundary lines, subtle ambient glow, bold typography, glowing crimson CTA buttons).

## Evidence on Hand

## Product Principles
1. **Show, Don't Promise:** Anchor every claim in real production systems, measurable metrics, and shipped case studies.
2. **Frictionless Conversion:** Every surface naturally leads qualified leads to the 15-minute discovery call.
3. **Editorial Conviction:** Confident, concise copywriting that speaks directly to founder pain points without fluff.
4. **Architectural Craft:** The portfolio itself is proof of technical excellence, responsiveness, and aesthetic precision.
